為什麼建立這個網站？ ｜ Our Story

我本身不吃素，但我深深愛著一位吃「蛋奶素」的伴侶。過去，我們常常為了找到一家能讓兩個人都開心吃飽、好好坐下來聚一聚的餐廳而傷透腦筋。

吃素的人要和葷食朋友一起吃飯，或者只想單純找個能好好共度時光的地方，真的比想像中還要困難。我知道那種在路上找不到餐廳、甚至在人生地不熟的地方旅遊時，必須一家一家點開 Google Map 確認到底是開是關的挫折感。

這張共享地圖我默默經營了幾年，現在希望把它開放出來，幫助每一個有相同困擾的人。我特別設計了「綠色代表營業、灰色代表休息」的功能，就是希望讓大家不再為吃飯卡關。只想讓找餐廳這件事，變得更簡單。

I'm a non-vegetarian, and I fell in love with a partner who is a lacto-ovo vegetarian. It used to be incredibly hard for us to find a restaurant where we could eat happily together. Vegetarian individuals face a real challenge trying to dine with non-vegetarians, simply looking for a place to share a meal and spend quality time together.

I know the deep frustration of hunting for a spot, especially when traveling in the middle of nowhere, having to click restaurants one by one just to check their hours. That's why I created this map—using Green for Open and Light Grey for Closed to make it effortless.

I have built and maintained this map for a few years, and now I hope to share it to help everyone who needs it. Just to make spending time together easier.
